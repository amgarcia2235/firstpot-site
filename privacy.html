Privacy Policy

Effective November 22, 2026

Short version: First Pot does not collect any data. There is no account, no server of ours, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Everything the app keeps stays on your iPhone or iPad.

What the app stores, and where it stays

On your device only:

  • Whether you are cooking for one or for two.
  • For each recipe you finish, whether you said it worked or something went wrong.
  • A cook in progress (the step you are on and any running timers), so an interruption does not lose your place.
  • Whether the app has already asked you for a rating in this version.

The app does not upload, sync or share any of this. Deleting the app deletes it.

What the app connects to

  • Apple, for the one purchase and to check what you have bought. Apple handles the payment; the developer never sees your payment details or your Apple Account.

Nothing else. The recipes and photos are inside the app, so it works with no connection.

Alarms and notifications

If you allow it, First Pot uses the system's alarms for one thing: to ring when a cooking timer you started finishes, even if your phone is locked or on silent. If you do not allow alarms, it uses a notification instead, if you allow those. Both are set on your device. No service of ours is involved, and nothing is sent to us.

Feedback you choose to share

When you finish a recipe you can share a short note about how it went. This uses the standard share sheet: you choose where it goes, and nothing is sent unless you send it.

Children

First Pot is not directed at children and collects nothing from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions: open an issue at github.com/amgarcia2235/firstpot-site/issues.
