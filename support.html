Support

Get in touch: most answers are below. If yours isn't, or a recipe didn't work for you, open an issue at github.com/amgarcia2235/firstpot-site/issues. We reply within 48 hours.

A recipe didn't work

Tell us which recipe, whether you cooked for one or for two, and what happened: too dry, too wet, not cooked through, too salty. Check the "If something goes wrong" section first. It's on each recipe page, and in cook mode under Ingredients. We fix recipes based on these reports.

Have the recipes been tested?

Each recipe page says where its amounts come from. The amounts for two were checked against several published versions of the dish. The amounts for one are scaled down from those, then adjusted where a straight half doesn't work. A small pot of rice, for example, needs a little more broth per cup.

The purchase

  • What it is: one purchase that gets you the ten recipes beyond the five free ones. It's not a subscription.
  • Restore: on a new phone, open any locked recipe, tap Unlock, then Restore purchase. It goes by your Apple Account. There's no First Pot account.
  • Family Sharing: everyone in your Family Sharing group gets the recipes too.
  • Refunds: Apple handles refunds at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Timers

A timer keeps ringing in the app until you dismiss it. To hear it when your phone is locked or you're in another app, allow notifications when the app asks on the get-ready page, or later in Settings > Notifications > First Pot. If notifications are off, keep the cooking screen open.

Food safety and allergies

Cooking times are a guide, not a promise. Check meat with a thermometer: 165°F for chicken, 160°F for ground meat and sausage. Each recipe lists the common allergens it contains, but brands differ, so check your labels.
