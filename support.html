Support

Get in touch: most answers are on this page. For anything else, or to tell us a recipe did not work, open an issue at github.com/amgarcia2235/firstpot-site/issues. We reply within 48 hours.

A recipe did not work

Tell us which recipe, whether you cooked for one or for two, and what happened (too dry, too wet, not cooked through, too salty). Each recipe page has an "If something goes wrong" section, and the same list is in cook mode under Ingredients. We fix recipes from these reports.

Have the recipes been tested?

Each recipe page says where its amounts come from: the amounts for two were checked against several published versions of the dish, and the amounts for one are scaled from them.

The purchase

  • What it is: one purchase that unlocks the twelve recipes beyond the three free ones. It is not a subscription.
  • Restore: on a new phone, open any locked recipe, tap Unlock, then Restore purchase. It uses your Apple Account; there is no First Pot account.
  • Family Sharing: the purchase is shared with your Family Sharing group.
  • Refunds: Apple handles refunds at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Timers

A timer keeps ringing in the app until you dismiss it. To hear it when the phone is locked or you are in another app, allow notifications when the app asks on the get-ready page, or later in Settings > Notifications > First Pot. With notifications off, keep the cooking screen open.

Food safety and allergies

Cooking times are guides. Check meat with a thermometer: 165°F for chicken, 160°F for ground meat and sausage. Each recipe lists the common allergens it contains, but brands differ, so check your labels.

Photos

The photos are stock photos that show the kind of dish, not our own cooking. Each recipe page credits the photographer.
